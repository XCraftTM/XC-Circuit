# How XC™ Circuit is made

**XC™ Circuit is designed and directed by XCraftTM and built with AI assistance (Anthropic's Claude).**

We think you should know that before you use it, so here's what that means.

## Who does what

- **XCraftTM** decides what XC Circuit is and does: which features it gets, how they should look and feel, what's good enough. Every feature is tried, and problems found by using the app are sent back until they're right.
- **Claude** (an AI) writes most of the code, the documentation and the bundled art and sounds (icons, the desk pet, the sound effects), following those decisions.

## What we do about quality

- **Tests:** more than a hundred automated tests for the editor and runtime run before every release, plus checks of the real interface rendered offscreen.
- **Open to read:** the [source code](https://github.com/XCraftTM/XC-Circuit) is public, so anyone can see how it works.
- **Real use:** bots are tested against real Discord and Fluxer servers before a release.
- **Maintained:** bugs are fixed and reported crashes looked at. Report anything with Help › Report a Bug.

## What it means for you

- Your bots, projects and plugins are **yours**. XC Circuit doesn't use AI while you build or run bots, and nothing you make is sent anywhere.
- XC Circuit has **no telemetry**. It only goes online to check for updates (you can turn that off) and when your bot runs.

If you'd rather not use software made this way, we understand. If you do use it, we'd love to hear what you build.
